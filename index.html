Our Story

A kitchen built around one flame.

Arshia Restaurant began with a single wood-burning hearth and a simple belief: the best food is honest, seasonal and touched by fire.

We work with eleven small farms within 80 miles of our door. Our oak is sourced from managed forests, our bread is proofed for 48 hours, and our menu changes with the market every week.

Marco Ellison

Executive Chef & Founder
